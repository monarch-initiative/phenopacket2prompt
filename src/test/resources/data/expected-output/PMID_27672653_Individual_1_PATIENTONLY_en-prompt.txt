The proband was a 20-year-old man. Disease onset occurred when the proband was a 5-month-old.
He presented with Acute encephalopathy, Elevated urine 3-hydroxyglutaric level, Glutaric aciduria, Inability to walk, Dystonia, Enlarged sylvian cistern, Seizure, Macrocephaly, Irritability, Subdural hemorrhage, and Absent speech. However, the following features were excluded: Cerebral atrophy, Hydrocephalus, and Developmental regression.
